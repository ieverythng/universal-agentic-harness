## Trajectory research programme

Neural Workbench's research objective is a trajectory laboratory that retains
complete attempts, compares alternative mechanisms, uses execution feedback to
shape later search, and evaluates reusable techniques. The AB/ML-object
architecture is a central strand of this programme. Context views and text
retrieval do not replace typed composition, representation search, or
parameter and delta search.

Harness2 provides source-observed fixed-weight search over editable harness
files, with candidate contrasts and scoped experience reuse. UniiChat provides
original-message retention and derived chronological views. Neither supplies
UAH authority, complete effect reconciliation, or an aggregate cost boundary.
The native Workbench source inspected for the dossier records a concrete open
feedback gap: its client does not retrieve stored traces or send history
signals to scoring, the scorer defaults those signals to zero, and macro
discovery groups repeated operator sequences without establishing causal
usefulness or promotion. Full source paths and hashes are in the dossier; the
native repository's inspected commit is `5983a6e1f53c47b6980a1f0afdeda398d5b9bb35`.
Public availability of its file URLs was not established.

## Existing matched-budget comparisons

The six arms A–F below reproduce proposals from the original research unit and
map them to existing sprint gates. No new experiment, threshold, seed, paid
run, or claim of statistical power is introduced here.

| Arm | Control, intervention, and falsifier | Gate placement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A, context | Flat original-only selection versus a derived chronological view, with the same eligible originals and auxiliary/context budget. Reject lost obligations or counterexamples, foreign retrieval, or no held-out benefit after all costs. | SPR-03/04 first establish deterministic preservation. The view comparison is a later model-quality study, not H1 completion evidence. |
| B, feedback | Strong fixed harness versus independent samples versus feedback-guided INV, DOM, or trajectory candidates under the same parent budget. Reject gains explained only by sampling or evaluator contamination. | SPR-05/06 accounting and conformance, then SPR-10/09 provenance and host projection. Broader H3 research. |
| C, selection | The same cached candidate pool with finals-only versus incumbent-inclusive and abstaining selection. Reject unsafe or worse adoption, hidden cost, or oracle-inconsistent preference. | Deterministic verification and admission first; SPR-10 selection and SPR-09 host. A cached pool does not measure end-to-end proposal quality. |
| D, experience | No library versus prior-prefix scoped experience versus a budget-matched unscoped library. Reject future leakage, retrospective-only benefit, or near-duplicate memorization. | SPR-03/04 eligibility and prefix closure, then qualified SPR-10/09. Broader H3 experience comparison. |
| E, recovery | The same fake-owner workload with settlement loss, duplicate ingress, wait/restart, cancel/late-result, and reduced context. Reject duplicate or unowned effects, invented settlement, or missing assigned trials. | SPR-01/03/04/05 contracts and SPR-06 conformance, then H2 or host-specific qualification. This is not a model-quality claim. |
| F, attribution | Component removal or substitution with matched reoptimization budget, retained failures, and disjoint holdouts. Reject sampling or other confounding, or a holdout benefit that vanishes. | SPR-06 evaluation hygiene and qualified engine/host evidence. H4 promotion discriminator, not frequency-based H3 BASE crystallization. |

For every arm, retain assigned, completed, graded, and censored denominators;
source, task, and oracle identities; auxiliary, child, and retry costs; and
unknown outcomes. Forward-prefix, frozen-holdout, and retrospective-library
analyses are distinct experiments. Unknown settlement and cost remain unknown.
Published call counts are not UAH spend admission.

## Source identity and open boundaries

The dossier pins Google Research [Harness2 at
`1cfe254e8d25b15f37048c3730134686abe60335`](https://github.com/google-research/harness2/tree/1cfe254e8d25b15f37048c3730134686abe60335),
[UniiChat at `0500bf6a8ea4bf0ce047488562022b1b6532cc30`](https://github.com/VictorTaelin/uniichat/tree/0500bf6a8ea4bf0ce047488562022b1b6532cc30),
and the Harness² paper at [arXiv:2610.14224v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14224v1).
It distinguishes the earlier [Harness Learning paper at
arXiv:2609.35738v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35738v1), which describes a
trained proposer and fixed solver, from fixed-weight external search. Actual
GRPO training requires a separate checkpoint, sampling, reward, and optimizer
contract. No training or benchmark was run for this proposal.

The supplied FPV-FAF archive is a future domain discriminator, not a formal
repository, flight implementation, or simulator result. Its archive and
extraction-manifest hashes and coverage limits are recorded in the dossier.
No simulator setup, hardware claim, or change to the NAO-first qualification
sequence follows.

The dossier separately records a possible native NAO/Workbench documentation
repair. That proposal needs a later owner scope, independent review, and human
publication; it is outside this PR2 research fold-in. Neither dossier nor
roadmap adopts upstream runtime code or changes H0, H1, H2, H3 BASE, or H4
authority.
