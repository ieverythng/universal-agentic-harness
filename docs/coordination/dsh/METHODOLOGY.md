## 3. Finite missions and local delegation

GRILL grills ambiguity, then dispatches one consolidated contract to the actual
branch parent. Standing ability to receive future authorized work is not an
unlimited work budget, an idle Goal loop or a per-mission messaging lease.

```text
Mission: unique task identity + correlation nonce
Owner: existing parent, approved local capacity/delegation
Objective: one bounded outcome and artifact contract
Inputs: governing rules, exact source/base and evidence references
WriteScope: exact paths/actions, destination base bytes and dependencies
NonGoals: prohibited effects and scope limits
Settings: selected model/optional effort/preset/permissions; known unknowns
Budget: finite time/work/capacity and stop condition
Acceptance: explicit ACK, terminal result, checks and owner evidence
Escalation: concrete decision required from parent/coordinator
```

Parents own their boards, integrate child outcomes and remain accountable for
cleanup and omissions. Descendants report to their own parent; consolidated
cross-branch dependencies return through GRILL. DEV and RESEARCH do not contact
or delegate directly into each other. An explicitly human-authorized DEV1/DEV2
technical handoff stays inside its assigned scopes and grants no review authority.
A manually operated research parent may opt in through its board/approved channel;
opt-in does not let another branch rewrite its board or commandeer its Session.

Use native Agent Teams/task boards first; verify actual parent Tools and capacity.
Missing capability requires an owner decision. Ordinary subagents require an
explicit justified fallback, never a substitute represented as a configured parent
or native Team. Do not enable plugins or force settings. Each local `lead` owns
its flat Team, not automatically GRILL; use current-revision task transitions and
collect explicit member/task results. Registration/inactivity is not completion.

Task revisions/dependencies and disjoint write scopes coordinate work but are
advisory, not locks, sandbox isolation or independent review. Protect dirty/staged
human bytes, verify destination baselines and use managed isolated worktrees.
Establish independent Git metadata or proven hook-local isolation before changing
hooks; never install hooks through a shared human common directory. A clean
review context does not require copying whole dependency trees.

## 4. Delivery, observation and uncertainty

Follow the bootstrap's four-Tool contract; do not reimplement transport through
boards, receiver jobs, a new bus, watcher, lease or raw user/owner RPC.

**Steer defaults both directions**, including mission dispatch and ACK/result
replies, at the next safe step—not mid-Tool preemption. Queue only for an explicit
request/reason, with a normal next-turn claim. Do not resend an already accepted
queued item merely to change mode. A passive own-inbox wait matches approved
peer relay source plus nonce, preserves entries and removes subscription/timer/
abort listeners on completion, timeout or disposal. A marker is not an ACK or
terminal result; claim/read the actual peer message normally.

Keep admission, inbox acceptance/flush, observation, explicit ACK, terminal result,
owner task acceptance, exact source review, installation, GUI qualification and
lifecycle/release closure separate. `whenIdle`, a local Team wait and an inactive
parent cannot decide unrelated-root mission completion.

Canonical post-effect `ABORTED` remains an error with no success value; a body
receipt may disappear after delivery. Report delivery unknown/may already have
happened and **do not retry blindly**. Never invent a receipt/message ID,
non-delivery, exactly-once or independent-worker cancellation guarantee. Known
before-dispatch refusal is distinct. Supported rollback changes code/config,
not past inbox effects. On failure/exhaustion, quiesce owned work, collect jobs
and return a precise blocker; retain evidence without replaying work automatically.

Changed bindings, Host restart/unload or unavailable effective Tools require
supported owner-approved recovery and an actual fresh handshake. Do not repair
missing proof by changing presets/efforts/permissions, creating replacement roots,
searching transcripts, spoofing user/Goal intent or marking a board complete.

### Current delivery record

GRILL maintains the single [current delivery record](<../../plans/current_delivery_state.json>)
during documentation sync and actual sprint or PR acceptance, staging, commit, push,
merge, qualification or rollback closeout. Fact issuers retain authority: check the

staging, publication and qualification claim with its owner, approved relative citation
and observation time. Use unknown or conflict when evidence is missing or inconsistent;
never infer one axis from another or treat a dated staging receipt as a live index query.
Keep cumulative path counts distinct and overlapping. Update the record as-of time,
verify the Explorer resource and rendered view by opening or manually refreshing it,
and record the outcome and any unavailable or stale-source gap. A read time is not
an evidence as-of time. Preserve dated plans and campaign receipts as history; do not
rewrite them or use this projection as authority for execution, publication or gates.
No polling or background updater is implied.

Repository architecture, execution and evidence rules remain with the governing
instructions linked above. This operational method does not redefine them or
turn coordination/research results into accepted implementation or release truth.

Each parent retains its owner-maintained board. Board writes do not wake another
root, authorize effects or extend deadlines. Preserve specifically authorized
verbatim continuing Codex records and provenance under the bounded publication
exception; that is not blanket permission for private source, Session/probe data
or new boards. After the human confirms a reviewed board cutover, use the agreed
persistent base rather than creating another canonical copy in the dirty checkout.

Freeze exact source/dependencies and follow the review contract: fresh independent
nonwriter contexts, predeclared inputs, baseline comparison, actual checks and
the required different-model review for high-risk gates. Record configured
model/effort and deviations; unattested backend execution is not a Max claim.
Writer self-check is not approval, and fixes need fresh review.

For major LLM-facing wording, lock objective, TRAIN inputs, unread independent
HOLDOUT and acceptance; record baseline → candidate → holdout → accept/reject.
Use bounded mutations, not accumulating prompt patches. A structural walkthrough
is not an empirical benchmark or training uplift. Publication requires exact
independent approval, repository checks and the human's explicit current action/
branch/path authorization; the human presently retains commits/pushes. Merge,
tags, release qualification and cleanup have their own gates. No plugin install,
private binding change or source transfer is authorized by this method alone.
