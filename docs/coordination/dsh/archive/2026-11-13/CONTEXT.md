# Coordination glossary

- **Role parent** — the actual independently addressable thread accountable for an organizational branch.
- **Team** — a Lead and its local helpers; not the whole organization.
- **Standing availability** — permission to receive bounded missions until revocation.
- **Mission** — a finite assignment with scope, capacity, acceptance evidence and stop condition.
- **Admission** — transport acceptance, not receipt of a completed result.
- **ACK** — the recipient’s explicit acknowledgement of a particular mission.
- **Current board** — small present-tense owner view of active scope and decisions.
- **Archive** — preserved completed evidence, not a replacement for current status.
